Featured work

Isaac Sim Pizza Scooping

n-fuse GmbH, Germany — 2023–2024

A robotic manipulator trained to scoop, drop, and handle pizza — both baked and raw. The raw dough was modeled as a deformable object and the scooping policy trained with reinforcement learning (Isaac Sim, PyTorch, Omni Isaac Gym Envs).

DEMETER — 4 AUVs, 60 Offshore Wind Turbines

Royal Holloway, University of London — 2022–2023

An Innovate UK consortium project (with Autonomous Devices, Signaloid, D-RisQ, CRP Subsea, and the Universities of Manchester and Strathclyde) moving subsea inspection from manual, low-frequency retrieval to automated, on-demand data collection. I worked on multi-agent task and mission planning, simulated in ROS, Gazebo, and ROSPlan.

Isaac Sim · MoveIt Pick & Place (UR3e)

Microsoft — 2024–2025

MoveIt-based pick-and-place motion planning for a UR3e arm inside Isaac Sim. Broader project details are under NDA.

ICRA 2020 — Segregation in Curves

PhD research, UFMG — 2020

A decentralized control strategy for segregating heterogeneous robot swarms along open curves, using formation control and a segregation heuristic. Code ↗
